Recent data from the Greater Kampala Metropolitan Area shows strong growth in residential property values, especially for standalone houses and well‑located apartments. While the market remains cautious after recent elections, demand is rebounding and quality homes are attracting serious buyers.

Why Sellers Are Winning in 2026

Properties near improved routes such as the Northern Bypass interchanges and the Kampala–Jinja Expressway corridor have seen some of the fastest appreciation, thanks to better access and reduced travel times.

Shift to the Suburbs

We are seeing a strong shift into secondary suburbs like Kyanja, Naalya, Bulindo and other emerging neighbourhoods. If your home is in one of these growing hubs, you are sitting on a high‑demand asset.

Middle‑Class Demand

Demand for 2–3 bedroom homes remains resilient, driven by high‑income Ugandan nationals, professionals and diaspora buyers looking for secure, well‑finished properties.
