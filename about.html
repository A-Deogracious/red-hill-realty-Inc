Red Hill Realty Inc. – Kampala’s Boutique Property Experts

Blending local knowledge, data, and personalized service to help you buy and sell with confidence across Greater Kampala.

Who We Are

Red Hill Realty Inc. is a boutique real estate firm focused exclusively on Greater Kampala and Central Uganda. We are not trying to be everywhere; we are dedicated to knowing this market better than anyone else.

Our team combines on‑the‑ground experience in neighbourhoods like Kololo, Muyenga, Kira, and Kyanja with modern data tools and digital marketing. This allows us to advise clients with real numbers, real comparables, and real opportunities — not guesswork.

Where Our Clients Love to Live

Connected by Accessible Roads

Many Red Hill Realty Inc. listings sit just off upgraded, all‑weather roads, giving you smooth access to schools, malls, offices, and the city centre without the stress of bad traffic bottlenecks.

Secure Gated Communities

From Garuga to Kira, we specialise in homes within gated estates that offer 24/7 security, organised neighbourhoods, and shared amenities that give families peace of mind.

Character‑Filled Colonial Land

We also represent historic, colonial‑style properties on generous land — perfect for boutique guesthouses and short‑stay retreats that attract tourists looking for an authentic Ugandan experience.

Our Mission & Values

Our mission is simple: to help Ugandans and diaspora investors make smart, secure property decisions that build long‑term wealth.

We believe in three core values:

  • Integrity in every deal.
  • Transparency in pricing, process, and paperwork.
  • Service that feels human, not purely transactional.

Every listing, showing, and negotiation is handled as if we were buying or selling for our own family.

Why a Boutique Firm?

In a crowded market of generic brokers, Red Hill Realty Inc. is intentionally small and specialized. That means:

  • You work directly with experienced agents, not a call center.
  • Your property is never “just another listing”; it receives a tailored strategy.
  • We move faster, communicate clearly, and adapt to your goals.

From first‑time home sellers in Naalya to seasoned investors assembling portfolios in Kigo or Naguru, our boutique model lets us give each client more time, more attention, and more insight.
