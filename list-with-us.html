Prepping Your Kampala Home for Sale

First impressions in Kampala can add millions of shillings to your final sale price. A buyer viewing a home in Kyanja or Muyenga decides within minutes whether they feel “at home” or are already looking for faults.

  1. Make essential repairs. Fix visible issues like damp patches, ceiling leaks, cracked perimeter walls, broken tiles, and rusty gates. Handling these before viewings gives buyers fewer excuses to demand heavy discounts.
  2. Clear the clutter. Remove extra furniture, piles of clothes, and personal items so rooms feel larger and cleaner. Keep the boys’ quarters, store, and garage just as tidy as the main house.
  3. Let in the light. Replace dim bulbs with bright LED lighting and open curtains or blinds during the day. Bright, naturally lit rooms photograph better and feel more secure and modern.
  4. Deep clean and refresh paint. A fresh coat of soft white or cream paint can make an older home in Bugolobi or Lubowa feel brand new. Pay extra attention to bathrooms, kitchens, verandas, and eliminate any damp or pet odours.
  5. Showcase the space. Arrange furniture to make movement easy and highlight the best features — views, balconies, gardens, or open‑plan living. Use fewer pieces in smaller rooms to avoid a cramped feel.
